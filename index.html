🌍 About the Project

Climatrix is an AI-based Climate Change Impact Prediction System developed at Galgotias University. It provides accessible, hyper-regional climate intelligence for India — delivering machine learning-driven forecasts and actionable insights for communities, farmers, planners, and policymakers.
